# Pilot Testing Survey

Copy these questions into Google Forms, Microsoft Forms or any survey tool.

## Form Title

DanceTime Pilot Testing Feedback

## Form Description

Thank you for testing DanceTime. Please answer based on your assigned role and the scenario you completed. Your answers will be used only for academic project evaluation.

## Questions

1. **Tester ID**  
   Short answer. Example: T1, T2, T3.

2. **Which role did you test?**  
   Multiple choice:
   - Client
   - Organizer
   - Admin
   - Validator

3. **Which device did you use?**  
   Multiple choice:
   - Laptop / desktop
   - Mobile phone
   - Tablet

4. **Which browser did you use?**  
   Short answer. Example: Chrome, Edge, Safari.

5. **Were you able to complete your assigned scenario?**  
   Multiple choice:
   - Yes, fully completed
   - Partially completed
   - No, could not complete

6. **How easy was the website to use?**  
   Linear scale 1-5:
   - 1 = Very difficult
   - 5 = Very easy

7. **Was the ticket booking process clear?**  
   Multiple choice:
   - Yes
   - Partially
   - No
   - Not applicable to my role

8. **Was the difference between reservation and full ticket purchase clear?**  
   Multiple choice:
   - Yes
   - Partially
   - No
   - Not applicable to my role

9. **Did you notice any error, slow loading or confusing step?**  
   Paragraph answer.

10. **What did you like about the system?**  
    Paragraph answer.

11. **What would you improve?**  
    Paragraph answer.

12. **Can your feedback and screenshots be used anonymously in the project report?**  
    Multiple choice:
    - Yes
    - No

## Recommended Evidence

Ask participants to upload or send screenshots separately:

- event page;
- reservation confirmation;
- ticket page with QR/barcode;
- organizer dashboard;
- admin panel;
- validator scan result.
