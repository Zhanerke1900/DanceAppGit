# Pilot Testing Checklist

Use this checklist when asking participants to test the DanceTime website.

## Before Testing

- Open production website: https://dance-app-git.vercel.app/
- Prepare tester code: T1, T2, T3, etc.
- Tell the participant which role to test: client, organizer, admin or validator.
- Ask the participant to take screenshots after important steps.
- Ask the participant to fill in the survey after finishing.
